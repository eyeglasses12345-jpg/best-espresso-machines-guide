## Product Role Rules

### Beginner Pages
Prioritize forgiving workflow, easy cleanup, consistency, and low frustration.

### Budget Pages
Prioritize realistic performance, grinder implications, durability expectations, and tradeoffs.

### Latte Pages
Prioritize steam power, milk workflow, repeatability, and cleanup burden.

### Built-In Grinder Pages
Prioritize convenience, counter-space savings, and all-in-one workflow.

### Automatic Pages
Prioritize convenience, consistency, daily use, and maintenance realism.

### Semi-Automatic Pages
Prioritize control, skill development, shot quality potential, grinder pairing, and long-term upgrade value.

### Compact / Small Kitchen Pages
Prioritize footprint, refill access, counter clearance, grinder placement, and storage reality.
