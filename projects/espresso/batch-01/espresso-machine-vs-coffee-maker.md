## HTML Description
<p>An espresso machine is usually better for people who want concentrated shots and milk-based café drinks. A coffee maker is usually better for people who want larger cups with the least routine complexity. If you want a middle path, systems like Nespresso Vertuo and Philips LatteGo can reduce friction while still giving a stronger café-style experience than basic drip coffee.</p>
<h2>The Real Decision Tension</h2><p>You are choosing beverage style and ownership effort at the same time. Espresso systems can deliver richer drink options, but they often ask for more maintenance attention than a simple coffee maker.</p>
<h2>Who This Page Is For</h2><p>People who like coffee at home and want to avoid buying the wrong category.</p>
