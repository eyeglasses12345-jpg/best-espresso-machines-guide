# Affiliate Category Template

# Purpose

This template defines:
# the reusable category architecture for all affiliate authority sites.

The goal is NOT:
- random affiliate articles
- disconnected reviews
- thin SEO content

The goal IS:
- semantic authority
- AI readability
- recommendation trust
- decision clarity
- ownership-fit matching

This template applies to:
- espresso machines
- nugget ice makers
- pizza ovens
- treadmills
- office chairs
- any future affiliate niche

---

# CATEGORY SYSTEM PRINCIPLE

Every affiliate category must behave like:
# a buyer-decision ecosystem.

NOT:
a collection of unrelated blog posts.

---

# REQUIRED CATEGORY STRUCTURE

Every affiliate category should contain:

1. Main Category Hub
2. Buyer-State Pages
3. Product-Type Pages
4. Use-Case Pages
5. Comparison Pages
6. Regret-Prevention Pages
7. Product Role Pages
8. Ownership/Maintenance Pages
9. Upgrade Path Pages
