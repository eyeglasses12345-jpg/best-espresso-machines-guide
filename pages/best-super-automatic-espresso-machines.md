<h2>What Buyers Usually Get Wrong</h2>

<p>The common mistake is assuming a superautomatic machine removes all effort. It reduces workflow friction, but it does not eliminate ownership responsibility. Cleaning, descaling, milk system maintenance, bean freshness, and water management still matter.</p>

<p>Another mistake is expecting superautomatic machines to behave like manual café equipment. These machines are built for convenience and repeatability first, not deep manual espresso control.</p>

<p>That is why two buyers can purchase the same superautomatic machine and have completely different experiences. One buyer loves the reduced workflow. Another buyer feels limited because they actually wanted manual shot control, grinder upgrades, and more hands-on experimentation.</p>
