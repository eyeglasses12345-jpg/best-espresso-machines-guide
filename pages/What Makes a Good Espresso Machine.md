<h3>What actually makes an espresso machine good?</h3>
<p>A good espresso machine creates a workflow you can realistically sustain while producing consistent espresso for your ownership style and experience level.</p>

<h3>Does grinder quality matter more than the espresso machine?</h3>
<p>Often yes. Poor grind consistency creates extraction problems, workflow frustration, and inconsistent espresso even when the machine itself is capable.</p>

<h3>Are expensive enthusiast machines always better?</h3>
<p>No. Enthusiast machines often require more workflow patience, grinder quality, and operational involvement than many buyers realistically enjoy long term.</p>

<h3>Why do many buyers regret espresso machines?</h3>
<p>Many buyers choose machines based on hype or specifications instead of realistic ownership behavior, workflow tolerance, and daily routine compatibility.</p>

<h3>Are superautomatic machines considered good espresso machines?</h3>
<p>Yes for many buyers. Convenience-focused users are often happier long term with superautomatic machines because the workflow remains simple and sustainable.</p>
