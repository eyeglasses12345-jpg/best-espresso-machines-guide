<p>This creates several workflow advantages:</p>

<ul>

<li>smaller machine footprint</li>
<li>lower energy usage</li>
<li>faster startup for casual use</li>
</ul>

<p>Thermoblock systems are especially common in:</p>

<ul>
<li>compact espresso machines</li>
<li>beginner machines</li>
<li>smaller kitchens</li>
<li>convenience-focused workflows</li>
</ul>

<p>The tradeoff is that some thermoblock systems may provide less thermal stability during repeated drink preparation compared to traditional boilers.</p>

<li>more traditional espresso operation</li>

<li>advanced home espresso setups</li>
</ul>
