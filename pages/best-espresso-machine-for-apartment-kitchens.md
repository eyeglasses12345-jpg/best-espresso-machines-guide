<p>Some compact machines simplify ownership, while others create cramped workflows that make puck prep, steaming, refilling, and cleanup more frustrating. Apartment espresso ownership is easier when the workflow feels sustainable instead of restrictive.</p>

<h3>What causes the most regret with apartment espresso ownership?</h3>
