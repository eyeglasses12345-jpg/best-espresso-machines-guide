<h2>What Coffee Lovers Usually Discover About Home Espresso</h2>

<p>Many coffee lovers initially assume the machine alone determines espresso quality. In reality, espresso is highly sensitive to grind consistency, fresh coffee, puck preparation, extraction timing, workflow repetition, and steaming technique.</p>

<p>That is why two buyers can purchase the same machine and have completely different experiences. One buyer enjoys refining technique and improving consistency. Another buyer becomes frustrated by grinder dialing, cleanup repetition, inconsistent extractions, or workflow fatigue and eventually stops using the machine.</p>

<p>Espresso machines are workflow systems, not simple kitchen appliances. The grinder, coffee freshness, puck prep, steaming workflow, water quality, and cleanup habits all work together.</p>
